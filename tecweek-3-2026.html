TECWEEK 3.0 — An Experience Worth Remembering!

IEEE ENIS Student Branch September 26 – September 27, 2026

I'm glad to have participated in TECWEEK 3.0, organized by IEEE ENIS Student Branch, bringing together passionate engineering students for two days of learning, innovation, collaboration, and networking.

I'm particularly honored to have been selected as Best Ambassador of the Event 🏆, a recognition that makes this experience even more meaningful.

Beyond the ambassador role, I also took part in the Technical Challenge, where I built GuardIA. It's an AI-powered campus security command center that brings live camera monitoring and real-time incident alerts, like fall and gas leak detection, together with student and classroom management in one dashboard. It was a great chance to put my technical and problem-solving skills to the test under pressure while collaborating with other talented participants.

A great experience filled with new connections, challenges, and unforgettable moments!

A big thank you to the organizing team and everyone who made this event possible. Looking forward to what's next! 🙌
